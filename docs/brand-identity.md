# Ovala Brand Identity

## Direction
Ovala should feel like a calm luxury product-photo studio: premium, minimal, image-led, precise, and trustworthy. The identity supports jewelry, gold, watches, and luxury accessories without becoming flashy or generic.

## Logo System
Source vectors are kept in `public/brand`.

Use:
- `public/brand/ovala-primary.svg` as the default stacked studio logo.
- `public/brand/ovala-horizontal.svg` where vertical space is tight.
- `public/brand/ovala-wordmark.svg` when the surrounding context already says studio.
- `public/brand/ovala-mark.svg` for compact brand moments.
- `public/brand/ovala-app-icon.svg` and `public/brand/ovala-favicon.svg` for square icon contexts.
- `public/brand/ovala-watermark.svg` only for quiet low-opacity marks on generated imagery.
- `public/brand/ovala-primary-light.svg` on warm light surfaces.
- `public/brand/ovala-primary-dark.svg` on deep dark surfaces.

## Typography
- Use Vazirmatn for app UI and body text.
- Use Doran only for very short display titles.
- Do not stretch UI text to imitate the logo.
- Keep Persian readable, direct, and UTF-8.

## Color And Material
- Warm ivory: `#F7F3EA`
- Soft black: `#11100E`
- Champagne gold: `#C7A96B`
- Stone taupe: `#B8AEA0`
- Mist gray: `#E6E1D8`

Use champagne gold sparingly for oval accents, divider lines, selected states, and quiet highlights.

## Image Direction
- Favor clean catalog/product photography, pale stone backgrounds, soft shadows, macro detail, and accurate material color.
- Source and generated images should feel inspectable, not decorative.
- Reuse oval crops, thin divider lines, and subtle frame guides when they support the workflow.
- Watermarks should sit quietly in a corner or neutral edge and never compete with the product.

## Avoid
- Neon gradients, robots, magic wands, chat bubbles, or obvious AI visuals.
- Diamond, crown, perfume, or fragrance references.
- Heavy black-and-gold luxury cliches.
- Nested cards, noisy borders, and generic SaaS dashboard presentation.
