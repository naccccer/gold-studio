# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ovala serves Persian-speaking jewelry, gold, watch, and luxury accessory sellers who need studio-quality product images from imperfect source photos. The admin console is used by operators and founders who need to monitor generation quality, payments, users, support, referrals, and provider health quickly.

## Product Purpose

Ovala turns low-quality product photos into premium catalog and campaign-ready images. The admin console exists to keep the system reliable before launch: identify operational risk, resolve user/payment issues, inspect generation failures, tune AI providers, and manage public catalog options.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, precise. The user app should feel premium and guided; the admin console should feel trustworthy, dense, and operational without becoming noisy.

## Anti-references

Do not make the admin feel like a decorative SaaS landing page, a card-heavy mock dashboard, a prompt playground, or a generic CRM. Avoid nested cards, random borders, noisy filters, perfume/fragrance metaphors, and unclear admin actions.

## Design Principles

- Lead with operational truth: show what needs attention first.
- Keep information architecture obvious after a few seconds.
- Prefer tables, detail pages, tabs, and action bars over decorative cards.
- Keep Persian and RTL behavior first-class.
- Preserve business boundaries: AI logic stays in `src/lib/ai`, business logic stays out of UI components.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible focus states, semantic controls, reduced-motion-safe interactions, and direct UTF-8 Persian text.
